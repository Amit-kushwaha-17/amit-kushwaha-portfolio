## Brand & Style
The design system delivers an adrenaline-fueled fusion of nocturnal street racing culture and futuristic cyberpunk aesthetics, framed by the surreal organic luminescence of midnight cherry blossoms. Designed for competitive sim-racers, telemetry enthusiasts, and arcade-tuning communities, it channels precision performance, illegal midnight time-attacks, and neon-lit atmospheric speed.

The visual direction merges **High-Contrast Cyberpunk** with **Tactile Automotive HUD Glassmorphism**. Dark carbon and obsidian surfaces ground the interface, while razor-sharp luminescent magenta accents cut through like neon taillights in a rain-slicked forest. Angular technical motifs, digital scanline accents, cockpit bracket framing, and precision telemetry dials evoke an authentic supercar telemetry terminal calibrated for late-night speed.

## Colors
The palette is built upon high-contrast emissive accents over deep void tones:
- **Primary (`#ff2d78` - Neon Blossom Magenta):** Drives primary actions, tachometer redlines, nitrous activation, and high-priority tactical HUD markers.
- **Secondary (`#00f0ff` - Electric Cyan / Xenon Blue):** Represents cold performance telemetry, active boost levels, shift indicators, and calibrated lock states.
- **Tertiary (`#ff66b2` - Petal Glow / Cherry Pink):** Provides radiant ambient glows, badge highlights, and secondary status confirmations.
- **Neutral Dark Base (`#0a0a0f` - Deep Obsidian):** Deep abyss black used for canvas backgrounds and viewport framing.
- **Surface Elevation (`#181824` - Carbon Weave):** Subtle tinted charcoal for panels, cards, instrument housings, and interactive modules.
- **Data Accents & Warnings:** Amber gold (`#ffb703`) mirrors ceramic-composite brake calipers and tire wear warnings.

## Typography
Typography reflects high-precision motorsport telemetry combined with digital HUD architecture:
- **Headlines (Space Grotesk):** Aerodynamic, geometric, and aggressive with tight tracking for section titles, race statistics, and telemetry dashboard headlines. Rendered uppercase for top-tier competitive telemetry.
- **Body Copy (Chivo):** Crisp, industrial grotesque providing maximum legibility under rapid glance conditions, high vehicle speeds, and dark mode reading.
- **Data & Telemetry Labels (Space Mono):** Fixed-width tabular figures used for lap split timers, RPM counters, speedometers (KM/H & MPH), gear ratios, and chassis diagnostic codes.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid 12-column telemetry grid optimized for dual cockpit HUD viewing and widescreen performance displays:
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `3rem` screen margins. Instrument clusters anchor symmetrically to the peripheral edges, leaving the central focal lane open for vehicle visualization or forward race perspective.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins. Gauge clusters collapse into side-by-side modular tiles.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins. Complex dial telemetry transitions to stacked horizontal progress gauges and modular cards.
- **Spatial Rhythm:** Tight inner-component padding (`0.5rem` to `1rem`) keeps high-density telemetry glanceable, while generous outer container gaps (`2.5rem`) maintain clean separation between active drive controls, diagnostics, and navigation.

## Elevation & Depth
Visual hierarchy is achieved through optical glow fields and dark tinted cockpit layers rather than conventional soft drop shadows:
- **Base Deck (Level 0):** Pure `#0a0a0f` void with subtle ambient radial gradients in `#ff2d7808` and `#00f0ff08` simulating distant neon headlights through dark cherry trees.
- **Telemetry Shells & Pods (Level 1):** `#181824` at 85% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a 1px border of `#ff2d7820` or `#ffffff10`.
- **Active Interactive Elements (Level 2):** Elevated cards framed with carbon composite gradient borders (`linear-gradient(135deg, #2a2a3e 0%, #12121a 100%)`) accented with sharp neon corner cuts.
- **Neon Glow Bleed:** Critical active states cast emissive outer glows:
  - Primary / Nitrous Glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px #ff2d7850, inset 0 0 10px #ff2d7825`
  - Secondary / Telemetry Lock: `box-shadow: 0 0 20px #00f0ff50, inset 0 0 10px #00f0ff25`
- **Scanline Overlays:** Subtle, semi-transparent horizontal scanlines (1px lines with 4px intervals at 3% opacity) can be overlaid across instrument panels for CRT racing cockpit authenticity.

## Shapes
The shape language relies on precision-machined chamfers, tight technical radii, and aerodynamic structural cuts:
- **Radius Tokens:** Base elements utilize `0.25rem` corners (`roundedness: 1`), conveying engineered carbon fiber panels and electronic hardware rather than playful app cards.
- **Chamfered Geometries:** HUD modules, achievement badges, and tactical buttons use 45-degree corner clips (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) on top-left and bottom-right edges to emulate high-performance digital displays.
- **Carbon Borders:** Thin 1px outer borders punctuated by technical tick marks and corner brackets `[ + ]` reinforce an instrumented cockpit vibe.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary (Nitrous Boost / Race Start):** Solid `#ff2d78` background with dark text (`#0a0a0f`), 45-degree clipped top-right corner, and dynamic cyan trailing underline. On hover, triggers an intensified emissive magenta corona glow.
- **Secondary (Telemetry / Tune):** Ghost button with 1px `#00f0ff` border, transparent dark fill, and electric cyan text. Hover state initiates a solid `#00f0ff20` fill with `0 0 16px #00f0ff60` neon edge bloom.
- **HUD Toggle Buttons:** Compact pill-less squares with monospace labels, illuminated status LED pips, and active state cyan borders.

### Telemetry Gauges & Speedometers
- **Radial Tachometer:** Segmented radial tick bars graduating from electric cyan (`#00f0ff`) through petal pink (`#ff66b2`) to neon magenta redline (`#ff2d78`). Large digital center readout in `Space Mono` bold.
- **Linear Boost & G-Force Meters:** Horizontal segmented bars using angled tick strips with glowing active segments and darkened depleted segments (`#1f1f2e`).

### Cards & Chassis Panels
- Enclosed in deep obsidian with subtle carbon weave textures. Accented with top-edge technical hairline borders (`#00f0ff40`) and glowing numeric quadrant indexes (e.g., `SEC_01 // CHASSIS`).

### Chips, Tags & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Low-profile monospace badges with semi-transparent tinted backgrounds (`#ff2d7815`), sharp 1px borders, and a pulsing 4px circular LED status indicator.
- **Achievement Badges:** Hexagonal or angled carbon shields with metallic inner borders, crowned with glowing neon cherry blossom filigree and electric cyan rank tiers (e.g., `APEX PREDATOR`, `MIDNIGHT DRIFTER`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Dark recessed input beds (`#0e0e16`) with bottom-only or sharp-framed 1px borders. Focused inputs activate an electric cyan boundary glow with high-contrast white text and fixed monospace unit indicators (`RPM`, `PSI`, `MS`).

### Lists & Leaderboards
- Row-based telemetry strips with alternating dark contrast banding, split-second deltas color-coded in cyan (gain) or magenta (loss), and driver rank numerals rendered in italicized `Space Grotesk`.
